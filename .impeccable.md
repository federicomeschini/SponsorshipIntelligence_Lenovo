## Design Context

### Users
Lenovo sponsorship, brand, finance, and senior leadership stakeholders reviewing the FIFA partnership in an executive presentation or focused desktop session. They need to understand whether the partnership is creating measurable value, what evidence supports that conclusion, and what the investment means financially.

### Brand Personality
Assured, tournament-scale, evidence-led. The product should carry FIFA's sense of global occasion without becoming fan merchandise, and Lenovo's analytical confidence without becoming a generic corporate dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, executive command brief with decisive dark-purple chapters and rare lime signals for the moments that matter. The visual language is editorial and data-forward: large claims, disciplined evidence, sharp geometry, and clear annotations. OpenEconomics typography and semantic tokens remain the identity system; FIFA is expressed through content, scale, event rhythm, and the exclusive focus on the partnership.

### Design Principles
1. FIFA is the product, not one property in a portfolio.
2. Lead with the decision case: financial return first, causal proof immediately beside it.
3. Make confidence legible: distinguish measured, modelled, and illustrative evidence at the point of use.
4. Use tournament energy selectively for milestones and conclusions; keep analytical surfaces calm.
5. Every screen should answer one executive question in under ten seconds.
